Privacy policy

What stays in your browser, what reaches the cloud, and the choices you have when using Atlas.

Who operates Atlas

This Privacy Policy describes Atlas, the free architecture-map website operated by Ahmed, based in Pakistan. For privacy questions and requests, contact mizuedit3@gmail.com.

Atlas helps you explore maps created from a coding agent’s findings. It does not read your repository or ask you to upload source repositories. A map can still contain sensitive information in its names, descriptions, paths or other fields, so review what you import and share.

Information used by the service

Using the website

Cloudflare hosts and delivers the website. Delivering and protecting a web request involves technical information such as the IP address, browser/device information and requested address. Provider processing is described in Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy. Local mode does not mean that opening a website makes no network requests.

Local maps and preferences

The application stores local maps, presentation preferences, workspace navigation, checkpoints and recovery records in browser storage. These records can include the content of a map you opened or edited. Downloaded backups and snapshot links contain the map information you choose to include.

Google sign-in

Optional sign-in uses Google and Firebase Authentication. Account information may include your account identifier, name, email address and profile-image URL supplied by Google, along with authentication/security information processed by the provider. Atlas stores a verified account profile with its identifier, name, email and timestamps, and account allowance/usage records for cloud workspace administration.

Google sign-in is for account access. It does not give Atlas access to your Gmail messages, Google Drive files or source repository.

Cloud maps

When you choose cloud storage, Atlas sends the map content to Google Cloud Firestore. This includes the project and component information, connections and optional details or measured charts contained in the map. Cloud metadata includes ownership, map identifiers, saved revisions, timestamps and sharing state. Support emails contain the information you send in the message.

Why information is used

Information is used to provide the features you request: displaying and editing maps, saving changes, restoring account access, sharing enabled maps, recovering drafts, managing limits and responding to support or privacy requests. Technical information is also used to operate and protect the service and comply with applicable obligations.

Signing in alone does not upload existing local maps. A cloud import, a deliberate move to Cloud Workspace, or an enabled future-map saving preference can upload map content. You can change that future-map preference in Settings.

The Atlas application has no advertising or analytics integrations and does not send maps to an AI service. Your separate coding agent handles your repository under its own permissions, terms and privacy practices.

Browser storage and sign-in

Atlas uses local browser storage and IndexedDB for maps, preferences, checkpoints, draft recovery and authentication persistence. The purposes differ:

  • Maps and backups: keep local work available in the same site/browser profile.
  • Preferences: remember choices such as theme, selected workspace and map presentation.
  • Checkpoints and drafts: preserve browser-held snapshots or pending work, including some work associated with a cloud account.
  • Authentication: Firebase can remember Google sign-in in this site’s browser profile until sign-out or storage removal.

Google’s sign-in flow can also use its own storage or cookies. Read Firebase’s privacy information and Google’s Privacy Policy for its processing.

Signing out does not delete local maps, downloaded files or all browser-held recovery records. Clearing site data can remove local maps and checkpoints and sign you out. Export important maps first. Browser storage is separate for different browsers, profiles and site addresses.

Providers and shared information

Google/Firebase provides authentication and optional cloud storage. Cloudflare provides hosting and delivery. If you email support, your email provider and the operator’s email service process the correspondence. These providers process information under their applicable terms and privacy practices.

Authorized administration uses account profile and quota records to operate the cloud service. Application permissions restrict access between users: owners can edit their cloud maps, and an enabled cloud share link provides view access to its holder.

Anyone with an enabled share link can read the shared map. Do not treat a live link as a private invitation. A local snapshot link carries its own copy of the map; its recipient can keep or change that copy. Cloud sharing can be stopped, but screenshots, files and copies already obtained cannot be recalled.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, protect the service or address a security issue. Atlas does not sell map content or Google account information.

Retention and deletion

Local information remains under the browser’s storage controls until removed by you, the application’s relevant deletion controls, browser settings or storage eviction. Checkpoint capacity is limited, and downloaded backups remain wherever you keep them.

Cloud maps remain until deleted through the service; the application does not use an inactivity-based expiry policy. Cloud deletion disables sharing first and then removes the workspace records and map data. A failed or interrupted deletion may need a retry. Stopping sharing alone does not delete the cloud map.

Deleting a map or signing out does not delete your authentication account or account profile. Contact mizuedit3@gmail.com for an account or personal-data deletion request. We may need to verify that the request is yours. Download information you want to keep before requesting deletion.

Operational, security, legally required records and provider backup copies may remain where needed under applicable obligations or provider retention practices. This policy does not promise instant removal from every backup or a fixed provider retention period. Support correspondence is retained as needed to deal with the request and related operational or legal needs.

Location and security

Atlas is operated from Pakistan and uses Google/Firebase and Cloudflare infrastructure. These providers may process information outside Pakistan or your own country. Operating Atlas from Pakistan does not mean all cloud data is stored in Pakistan.

Atlas uses the hosting and cloud services’ security features and application access controls. No online service or browser storage can be guaranteed completely secure or permanently available. Keep backups, use an appropriate browser profile and avoid placing credentials or confidential information in public links.

Your choices and requests

You can use local mode without signing in, choose whether to move or import maps to the cloud, change future-map saving, export maps, stop cloud sharing and delete maps you own. A recipient’s existing copy is outside those controls.

Depending on the law that applies, you may also have rights to request access, correction, deletion, portability or restrictions on certain processing, object to processing, withdraw applicable consent or complain to a relevant authority. Contact mizuedit3@gmail.com so the request can be assessed and your identity verified where necessary. Do not send a password or access token.

For practical steps, see local and cloud storage, backups and sharing in Help.

Changes and contact

This page will be updated when Atlas’s information practices change. Its date identifies the current version. Material changes will be explained where appropriate, and additional notice or choices will be provided when applicable law requires them.
